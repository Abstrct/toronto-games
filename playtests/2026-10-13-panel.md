# Toronto Games: playtest panel, 2026-10-13

Ten AI testers, all California Games fans from total beginner to expert, each with a persona.
Together they made about 600 runs on the real ROM using `tools/run.py`. Their condensed reports are in
`playtests/2026-10-13-panel/pt*.md`. Bot and fuzz numbers come from `tools/pt` and are labelled
"bots" wherever they appear.

**Verdict.** Every tester praised the art, the Toronto writing, the sponsor wall and the
Nathan Phillips Square podium. Every tester also found that the scoring doesn't reward
skill. In Kenji's words: *"The title screen is a real 16-bit game, and every screen after it needs to catch up."*

**Robustness is solid.** Nobody could crash it: Mo made 55 abuse runs, and the bots fuzzed for 2 hours
(12 runs of 10 minutes each) reaching every screen, with no crash, freeze or soft-lock.

## P0: fairness and blockers (fix before the next panel)

1. **Doing nothing beats playing.** All 10 testers hit this, and the bots confirm it.
   - **Bike:** holding A without steering scores 2942 while WIPED OUT, beating the 2500 record. The 17 tokens sit in that lane, and a steering bot scores 378.
   - **Tallboy:** holding B from frame 0 scores 1443–1550 in 7 s. Honest running scores 236–840. Rob's formula: score = 100×sips + distance/2 + 10×jumps.
   - **Root cause:** a failed run (DNF) can outscore a finished one. Make finishing worth more than any DNF: score wipeouts and busts as distance only, cap sips or scale them by distance, and give a finish bonus.
2. **Zero scores win medals; ties go to Player 1.** 9 of 10 testers.
   - Three players scored 0 in the Regatta and all three got silver, which decided the final standings.
   - A solo player gets gold for 0.
   - Identical scores are ranked by player number, and P1 is crowned Champion.
   - Fix: no medal for a score of 0, shared places for ties, and a visible tiebreak.
3. **A skips through cards.** Marcus (blocker), Priya, Rob, Dana.
   - A is jump in Tallboy, so a busted player who keeps mashing skips their own score, the event results and the *next player's intro*; the next turn starts without them.
   - In Practice, the same mashing lands you in name entry typing AAAAAAAA.
   - Fix: lock input on cards for about 1 s and require a fresh press.
4. **In the Regatta you can't get unstuck.** 9 of 10 testers.
   - Turning at the start runs you AGROUND on the Island within a second, and you sit there for up to 150 s with BUMPS 68–144.
   - Mark 1 sits on a pier piling: touching it means AGROUND unless you turn more than 120°.
   - Fix: auto-push off the shore after about 2 s, move mark 1, and cut the clock (the course takes 23 s for an expert, against a 150 s clock).
5. **Off-Leash collisions.**
   - Holding UP snags you on a bench or tree for 15–40 s.
   - You can walk off the bottom of the screen, under the HUD.
   - Fix: slide along obstacles and clamp the bottom edge.
6. **The results headers are wrong.**
   - Bike timeout says "FINISHED!" at distance 328.
   - Off-Leash timeout says "TACKLED!" with 0 tackles.
   - "MADE IT TO DUNDAS!" can show a card saying "TACKLED!".
   - Tallboy's JUMPS counter stays at 0 or 1 however many jumps you make.

## P1: make each event learnable and fun

| Event | The problem (by tester count) | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Regatta** (3.5) | The boat is 8–10 px, its heading can't be read, and the camera pins it to the screen edge. The mark arrow points straight upwind, so following it leaves you stuck at speed 0 with no message. Geese, buoys and kayaks look alike. **But Rob rated it 7, the only event that needs real skill: no-go zone, tacking, shifts.** | Keep the physics and add teaching. Use a bigger boat with a heading line and a no-go wedge, and an "IN IRONS: turn off the wind" popup. Centre the camera on the boat. Use one loop of the course and a 60–75 s clock. The Island ferry horn could be the start gun. |
| **Bay St** (5.2) | The card says "TAP IN TIME", but nothing shows the beat. Rob found the optimum is one step every 8 frames; 5 taps/s barely moves you. Coffee is gone in about 10 s, whatever you do. Sandwich boards and carts block your lane for 30–40 s. Changing lane means holding a direction on the same thumb that's alternating strides. | Add a visible and audible footstep metronome with a timing window ("good" or "perfect" steps). Make coffee loss scale with collisions, not speed. Have blockers step aside after "Excuse me!" and make a single tap change lanes. |
| **Off-Leash** (5.4) | "!" gives 12–25 frames of warning. The card says "B SIDESTEP", but plain B steps right, even into a dog coming from the right. A dog you dodge circles back and tackles you 40 frames later. "FETCH!" still gets you tackled. A yellow athlete disappears on yellow picnic blankets. | Make the warning about 40 frames and have the sidestep move away from the dog. Give dodged dogs a cooldown and show "DODGE!" feedback. | **Tallboy** (4.9) | Beyond the exploit: benches and crates come too fast to read, the play lane is a thin strip under 60% scenery, and you're busted in 6–20 s. | Telegraph obstacles and make the lane taller. | **Bike** (6.2) | Potholes look like manhole covers (5 testers). Steering into the left lane respawns you into traffic for 3 wipeouts in 6 s. Cars stack and drive through streetcars. Respawn into the bike lane with invulnerability. Fix the car spacing. Profile the slowdown. |

**Event length.** California Games turns ran about 60–90 s. Here Off-Leash and Tallboy
end in 6–20 s, Bay St is a fixed 60 s, and the Regatta is 150 s.
Marcus timed a 4-player game at about 25 minutes of machine time; with real people that's
about 35 minutes, and 60+ for 8 players.

## P1: the meta-loop

- **No retry or rematch.** After Practice it takes more than 9 presses plus a retyped name to play again. After the podium you land on the title screen and the roster is wiped. Add RETRY / NEXT EVENT / MENU on results, and "Rematch, same players" after the podium.
- **No pause.** START does nothing mid-event.
- **No medal table between events,** so the "who's winning?" drama only appears at the very end. Marcus suggests a CN Tower LED ticker.
- **Records are thin.** There's one entry per event, the seeded records are uneven (Bike is easy to beat, Bay St is hard), and results don't show how the score was built. Add a top-5 per event and a score breakdown.
- **Solo play has no stakes.** Tasha suggests turning the seeded record holders (COURIER, SUITGUY, RACCOON, GOOSE) into ghost rivals.
- **No attract mode,** and the sound test isn't signposted.

## P2: presentation bugs

- **Text spacing.** Two-line text has 0 px spacing on intro cards, records, competitor lists and results headers. Use a 16 px pitch.
- **Layering:**
  - Cars, dogs and sailboats draw over the HUD.
  - A pothole draws on top of a car.
  - "PRESS A" overlaps the podium blocks (6 testers).
  - Park banners sit under sprites.
- **Maple-leaf backdrop.** It scrolls off its tilemap, leaving a blank band that grows until the whole backdrop is empty: `w200 pSTART w40 s:a w200 s:b w300 s:c`.
- **Tallboy sky.** The sunset gradient turns flat cream when the bust or results card opens.
- **Clipping.** The last "O" of TORONTO is clipped on the title. "SAM THE RECORD MAN" overflows the competitor box.
- **Sign-up:**
  - A blank name is accepted and shows up as an empty record holder.
  - DEL on an empty name exits sign-up.
  - Compete in Some with nothing ticked starts the event under the cursor.
  - Duplicate sponsors make identical jerseys.
  - Jersey colours clash: Shopsy's card is yellow but its shirt is maroon; Honest Ed's and Mr. Sub share a yellow, Sam's and Canadian Tire a red.
  - The screen shows "PLAYER 1" but the stored default name is "PLAYER1".
- **Sprite size (Kenji).** Athletes are about 12×20 px with 2-frame cycles, and dogs are 10×8. California Games's big, expressive athletes were half its charm.
- **Audio (Kenji).** It's mono (L equals R), with no echo. Use the SPC's stereo and echo. "Gold Guns Gulls" plays in three events, so it wears out. The podium song is the flattest of the seven.

## P3: the soul gap (what California Games had)

Testers kept raising these, and most of them are design work, not bug fixes:

- **A style or trick layer.** Half-pipe and footbag rewarded named tricks. Here mastery means only "finish faster". Ideas: a Bike "track hop" or "drafting the 501", Tallboy sip-mid-air combos, Off-Leash frisbee catches (a nod to CG's flying disc), ferry-wake surfing.
- **A distinct verb per event.** Bike, Off-Leash and Tallboy are all "avoid the thing in your lane".
- **One obvious input on the first go, and physics that teach themselves.** CG surfing taught itself in 5 s.
- **Failure that's short and funny.** "I spent two and a half minutes aground on Centre Island. Very Toronto."

## Toronto

**What works (unanimous):**
- the sponsor wall copy ("Come in and get lost!", "967-11-11")
- the dog-owner lines
- "BUSTED! OPEN CONTAINER!"
- dooring and "CAUGHT A TRACK!"
- the 5:42 GO train
- the skyline title
- the Nathan Phillips podium

**Corrections (Gord, born and raised there):**
- **Streetcars should be TTC red,** not silver.
- **Bay St needs a real finish.** It should end at Union's columns with a GO train pulling out. Bay at King should feel like a canyon of towers.
- **Regatta shoreline.** The city shore needs the skyline and condo wall, not grass and a shed.
- **Music: decided, no change.** Gord noted that Mother Mother, Sam Roberts and the Hip aren't Toronto bands. The team is keeping the soundtrack as is: it's a Canadian soundtrack, and it's staying.
- **Progress labels.** K, US, DUN and SPA mean nothing to visitors. Spell out the stops.

**Most-requested additions:**
- raccoons (7 testers): steals your tallboy, a tennis ball, or a spot in the podium crowd
