- **Tallboy:** clearly pulls away. 1470 → 5118 (3.5x), but it's the same trick 26 times.

- **Regatta:** 666 (ADRIFT at the pin) → 2100 with one correct tack. Navigation, not style.
- **Bike:** TRACK HOP X2 chains appear, but I couldn't survive long enough to cash style.

## 5. Report sections
**Who:** a Leslieville lifer, TTC rider and Leafs/Jays sufferer.
**Overall 7/10:** for the first time a first-timer gets to the finish in most events (Off-Leash, Tallboy, Regatta, Subway, Bay in double time). vs CG 6.5/10.
**Per event:**
- Bike 6: the 501 warning is fair, but the pothole conveyor in the curb lane isn't fun.
- Bay 6: Scotia/TD/Commerce Court canyon and the street-meat cart are great; following the card misses the train.
- Off-Leash 7: Bellwoods courts, maples, the drum circle, and a fair "!".
- Tallboy 7: FRUIT/CHEESE/RECORDS/TACOS shopfronts, the raccoon record, finishable.
- Regatta 6: SkyDome rightly west of the Tower, ferry, laker, Island beach, auto push-off. "666" for my bumpy run was funny by accident.
- Parking 5: the ticket officer is spot on; the wheel lag and prompts fight you.
- Subway 7: Bloor-Yonge→Union order, platform signs, readable lurches.

**Top 3 loved:** TTC Surfing's interior and jokes; Off-Leash finally being fair; Kensington shopfronts plus the raccoon.

**Top 5 problems:** Bay card vs DOUBLE TIME [major]; Tallboy rote sip spam [minor]; tack coaching [minor]; Bike pothole spam in the curb lane (no id: a design gripe); Parking prompts [minor].

**CG had:** varied tricks, each worth learning. Here most style is one move repeated.

**More Toronto:** TMU/Dundas gag; "UNION! THIS TRAIN IS OUT OF SERVICE"; "MADE THE 5:42!"; a "501 SHORT-TURNED AT SHAW" fail line; the Horseshoe, Bovine and the CityTV building on Queen.

**Wall quote:** "First time I reached Union without touching a pole. Now give me a reason to go back for style."

## 6. As good as California Games?
Not yet, but close on fairness. The biggest thing in the way is **style depth**. Every event now lets a first-timer finish, but the high scores come from one rote trick (sip-sip-sip) or from simply surviving. California Games made you want to learn five tricks per event.
