# Toronto Games

A Super Nintendo game in the spirit of *California Games*: five events across
Toronto, 1–8 players taking turns, each with a name and a classic Toronto
sponsor. Written in 65816 assembly; all art and music generated by the
Python tools in `tools/`.

**ROM:** `toronto_games.sfc` (LoROM, 512 KB, battery SRAM for records). Runs
in any SNES emulator (Snes9x, bsnes, Mesen) or on a flash cart.

## The events

| # | Event | Where | Controls | Goal |
|---|-------|-------|----------|------|
| 1 | Bike Courier | Queen St W (top-down) | ◀ ▶ steer · A/▲ pedal · B/▼ brake | Ride to Spadina. Dodge opening car doors, cross streetcar tracks square-on, watch for the 501. Collect TTC tokens, earn near misses. 3 wipeouts and you're out. |
| 2 | Bay St Power Walk | Bay at King (side view) | Tap ◀ ▶ alternately to stride · ▲ ▼ change lane · A "Excuse me!" | Reach Union before the 5:42 GO train. Pass the crowd, don't spill the coffee. |
| 3 | Off-Leash Alert | Trinity Bellwoods (3/4 view) | D-pad walk · A throw tennis ball · B sidestep | Cross the park gate to gate. Dogs spot you and charge; dodge at the last second or send them after a ball. 3 tackles and you're done. |
| 4 | Tallboy Trot | Kensington Market (side-scroller) | A jump (hold for height) · hold B to sip | Outrun the bike cop to Spadina. Every sip scores but slows you, and the buzz bends the street. |
| 5 | Harbour Regatta | Toronto Harbour (top-down) | ◀ ▶ steer · A hike out in gusts | Round the three marks in order and cross the line. You can't sail straight into the wind: tack. Avoid the ferry, geese and kayaks. |

Scores feed the medal table (gold 5, silver 3, bronze 1). After the last
event the top three take the podium at Nathan Phillips Square. Each event's
best score is saved as a Toronto Record.

Menus: **Compete in all events**, **Compete in some**, **Practice one event**,
**Toronto Records**. Sign-up uses a letter grid (or Start for a default name)
and a sponsor wall: Honest Ed's, Sam the Record Man, Eaton's, Pizza Pizza,
Swiss Chalet, Mr. Sub, Canadian Tire, Roots, Laura Secord, Shopsy's,
Steam Whistle, MuchMusic. Your athlete wears the sponsor's colours.

## Music

Every track is an 8-bit loop after a Canadian band: the chords, tempo and
groove follow the original, and the melodies wander just far enough off.
Hear them all on the title screen's sound test (press SELECT).

| # | Track | After | Plays during |
|---|-------|-------|--------------|
| 1 | KC Incidental | Broken Social Scene, "KC Accidental" | title |
| 2 | Bobcaygeese | The Tragically Hip, "Bobcaygeon" | menus, sign-up |
| 3 | Gold Guns Gulls | Metric, "Gold Guns Girls" | Bike Courier |
| 4 | Ahead by a Centimetre | The Tragically Hip, "Ahead by a Century" | event standings, final table |
| 5 | Whelp, I'm Alive | Metric, "Help I'm Alive" | Bay St Power Walk |
| 6 | Hay, Officer | Mother Mother, "Hayloft" | Tallboy Trot |
| 7 | Brother Downwind | Sam Roberts, "Brother Down" | Harbour Regatta |
| 8 | If I Had a Million Collars | Barenaked Ladies, "If I Had $1000000" | Off-Leash Alert |
| 9 | Podium Reprise | the title theme behind a fanfare | podium at Nathan Phillips Square |

Every event has its own song. The standings song carries straight on into the
final table, and the podium brings the title theme back. The mix is stereo:
lead slightly left, harmony slightly right, with a short SPC echo on both.

Songs are written in `tools/songs.py` (one 16th note per token, NES-style
lead / harmony / triangle bass / drums) and compiled by `tools/audio.py`.

## Building

Needs `cc65` (ca65/ld65) and Python 3 with Pillow.

```bash
make
```

`make` regenerates the art and audio (`tools/build.py` → `build/*.bin`),
checks register-width bookkeeping (`tools/modecheck.py`), assembles
`src/*.s`, links with `snes.cfg`, and patches the header checksum.

## Layout

- `src/boot.s` reset, NMI (OAM/CGRAM/text/queued VRAM DMA), joypads
- `src/ppu.s`, `src/text.s`, `src/math.s` video, text and maths helpers
- `src/audio.s` SPC700 register-writer driver; sequencer and sound effects run on the 65816
- `src/game.s` game director, scoring, medals, SRAM records, shared helpers
- `src/title.s`, `menu.s`, `register.s`, `cards.s`, `ceremony.s` screens
- `src/ev_*.s` the five events
- `tools/art_*.py` pixel art for each screen, `tools/audio.py` samples + songs, `tools/font.py` text tiles

## Dev tools

- `python3 tools/run.py "<script>"` runs the ROM headless (snes9x libretro core in
  `build/emu`) with a button script and saves screenshots to `build/shots/`
  — e.g. `"w200 pSTART w40 s:menu"` (`wN` frames, `pBTN` tap, `hBTN`/`rBTN`
  hold/release, `s:name` screenshot, `c` CPU registers, `v:file`/`V` audio capture).
- `python3 tools/pt/pt.py` is the playtest harness: persistent, branchable play
  sessions with game telemetry (scene, score, fails, slowdown), films, bots and
  balance flags, fuzzing, golden-scene checks and bug filing with verified repros.
  See `tools/pt/README.md`; `make qa` runs the automated checks.
- `python3 tools/gallery.py` captures showcase screenshots into `build/gallery/`.
- `python3 tools/jukebox.py [seconds] [song ...]` records songs from the ROM's
  sound test into `build/music/NN.wav` (`JUKEBOX=--gram` adds a notegram PNG).
- `python3 tools/modecheck.py` flags branches into code assembled for the wrong
  accumulator/index width (also run by `make`).
